## Final Phase A refinements

On the authorised Xiaomi MiTV-AFKR0 / Android 11, a diagnostic build logged the misleading discovery toast as `LeftCompositionCancellationException: The coroutine scope left the composition`. Initial discovery was replaced by the resume-triggered refresh, and `runCatching` incorrectly treated cancellation as a failure. Replaced the competing triggers with one resumed-lifecycle refresh. Cancellation is rethrown; real exceptions are logged and shown as retryable state, preserving existing apps. Success clears the error. Three added unit tests cover cancellation and failure/recovery, including successful empty results.
